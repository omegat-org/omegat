Let fuzzy matching understand numbers

Manuals, price lists and reports repeat the same sentence with different numbers: "Tighten to 12 Nm" and "Tighten to 8 Nm", or segments that are nothing but a date or an amount. Normally such number-only segments find no fuzzy match at all.

Enable Consider numbers in fuzzy matching in the project properties, and the Fuzzy Matches pane starts comparing numbers by their value:

  • Number-only segments (dates, quantities, prices) are offered as matches for each other, with a small penalty.
  • Numbers count as equal across writing systems when their value is the same: XIV, 十四, ١٤ and the full-width １４ all equal 14.
  • A tooltip on every match shows how its score was computed — and warns you when the numbers of the match differ in value from your segment, so a wrong number never slips into your translation unnoticed.

The option is stored in the project, so the whole team benefits from it.
