Let fuzzy matching understand numbers

Manuals, price lists and reports repeat the same sentence with different numbers: "Tighten to 12 Nm" and "Tighten to 8 Nm" — and number-only segments (a date, an amount) normally find no fuzzy match at all.

Consider numbers in fuzzy matching in the project properties makes the Fuzzy Matches pane compare numbers by value:

  • Number-only segments (dates, quantities, prices) match each other, with a small penalty.
  • Equal value counts as equal across writing systems: XIV, 十四, ١٤ and the full-width １４ all equal 14.
  • A tooltip shows how each score was computed and warns when the match's numbers differ from your segment's — no wrong number slips in unnoticed.

Stored in the project, so the whole team benefits.
