Fill number-only segments automatically

Price lists, catalogs and financial reports are full of segments that contain nothing but a number, a price, a date or a time. Tools > Auto-convert Number Segments... fills them all in one go, written the way your target language writes them.

This is more than copying the source: German 1.234,56 € becomes English €1,234.56, English 12/31/2026 becomes German 31.12.2026, and Chinese 第3 becomes English 3rd. Roman, ideographic, Arabic-Indic, Devanagari, Thai and full-width numerals are read, too.

Every conversion is shown with a preview and a confidence value before anything is applied, and the converted segments are marked so the jump to the next untranslated segment skips them.
