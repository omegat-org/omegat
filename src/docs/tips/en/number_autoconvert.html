Fill number-only segments automatically

Price lists, spec tables, catalogs and financial reports are full of segments that contain nothing but a number, a price, a date or a time. Tools > Auto-convert Number Segments... fills them all in one go, written the way your target language writes them.

This is more than copying the source: the number is read in the source language and rewritten for the target language, whatever the pair. For example:

  • German 1.234,56 € → English €1,234.56
  • English 12/31/2026 → German 31.12.2026
  • Arabic ٥٠٪ → English 50%
  • Chinese 第3 → English 3rd, and English 1st → German 1.
  • Swedish 3:e → English 3rd

It also reads numerals of many writing systems: Roman MMMCCXIV, ideographic 三千二百十四, Arabic-Indic ٣٢١٤, Devanagari ३२१४, Thai ๓๒๑๔ and full-width ３２１４ all become the plain 3214 your target language expects.

Every conversion is shown with a preview and a confidence value before anything is applied, and the converted segments are marked so the jump to the next untranslated segment skips them.
