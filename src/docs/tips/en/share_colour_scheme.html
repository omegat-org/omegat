Share your colour scheme

In Options > Preferences > Colours, Export Scheme... saves all editor colours to a small, human-readable .properties file; Import Scheme... reads it back on any OmegaT 6.2 or later. Imported colours show up in the table right away and take effect with Apply or OK — Cancel drops them.

Plain text: open in any editor, tweak a value, or pass to colleagues so everyone works with the same look.
