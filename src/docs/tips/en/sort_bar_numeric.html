Sort segments by numeric value

The sort bar's direction selector also offers Numeric ascending and Numeric descending: segments are ordered by the value of the number they contain, so "Step 2" comes before "Step 10" — alphabetically, "10" would come first.

Recognized are signs, decimals and fractions (-5, 1.5, 3/2, ½) and numerals of many writing systems — Roman (XII), CJK (十二), circled numbers (⑩) and further systems supported by ICU. Values compare exactly: 1/3 orders correctly against any decimal, very large numbers never overflow, and segments without a number fall back to alphabetical order.

Words such as "mix" or "XL" are valid Roman numerals too, so Numeric asc./desc. no-Roman counts them as plain text — the collapsed sort bar shows 1↑/1↓ instead of #↑/#↓.
